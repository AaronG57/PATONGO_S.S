About the school

A government-aided secondary school in Patongo

Patongo Secondary School, commonly listed as Patongo S.S., is a government secondary school in Agago District.

The Uganda Ministry of Education and Sports' placement information identifies Patongo S.S. with placement code 2406.

The Ministry classifies the school as O-Level and Mixed/Day.

Government education records identify the institution with EMIS code 4153.

Current administrative records place the school in Agago District, while older records used the former Pader District designation before the creation of Agago District.

School history & records

Patongo S.S. in Uganda's public school records

The school's records span the period when Patongo was associated with the former Pader District administration and the current Agago District administration.

From Pader records to present-day Agago

Older Ministry of Education placement documents list Patongo S.S. under Pader. Current government school records list the institution under Agago District.

This reflects the administrative history of the area: Agago became a separate district from Pader in 2010.

The school's government-school listing gives it EMIS code 4153. A separate institution named Patongo Seed Secondary School has a different record and should not be confused with Patongo S.S.

Important distinction: Patongo S.S. and Patongo Seed Secondary School are separate schools. This page is specifically for Patongo S.S., EMIS 4153 and placement code 2406.

Research & verification

Sources behind this profile

Government records were prioritized for the school's identity, EMIS code, district, placement code and educational classification.

  • Uganda Ministry of Education & Sports Government secondary-school records list Patongo S.S. under Agago District with EMIS code 4153.
  • Ministry of Education Placement Information The Ministry's placement information lists Patongo S.S. as placement code 2406, O-Level and Mixed/Day.
  • Uganda EMIS Uganda's Education Management Information System is the government's education institution information platform and maintains institution profiles, ownership and location information.
  • Education Service Commission Education Service Commission records contain personnel-related references to secondary schools in Agago and separately identify Patongo Seed S.S., helping distinguish the two institutions.
  • Historical Public School Directory The 2016 East African Education Directory lists Patongo S.S.S. with P.O. Box 27 and telephone +256 772 311843.
  • Historical Ministry Records Earlier Ministry records list Patongo S.S. under the former Pader administrative designation, while current records use Agago District.
  • Patongo S.S. vs Patongo Seed S.S. Government records show that these are separate institutions with different school records. This profile concerns Patongo S.S., EMIS 4153.
  • Accuracy Policy Current enrollment, fees, facilities, examination scores and staff numbers have not been presented as current facts without adequate authoritative evidence.
